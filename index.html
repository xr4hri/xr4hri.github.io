Workshop plans

What the workshop is about

Humans and robots working together need a shared understanding of goals, intentions, and context. XR gives both a shared spatial medium: it can show a robot's planned motion before it happens, reveal its internal state, let people teleoperate from afar, or prototype robot behaviour before any hardware exists.

XR4HRI brings together researchers from human-robot interaction, robotics, HCI, and the XR community to discuss these systems, share early results, and try out each other's demos.

Technologies and concepts

  • Registration and tracking
  • Visualizing data, intent, communication, and feedback
  • Interaction concepts
  • Touch, haptics, and pseudo-haptics
  • Safety and risk management
  • Usability and user experience
  • Robotic virtual twins for teleoperation or simulation

Robots and application domains

  • Stationary, mobile, and flying robots
  • Functional, humanoid, and zoomorphic robots
  • Teleoperated, shared-control, and peer-to-peer settings
  • Industrial, construction, medical, and automotive applications
  • Assistive, consumer, and social robotics
